# SS-09 sponsorship kernel

Endpoint-owned sponsor commitments, operator-controlled acceptance/cancellation, and explicitly public sponsor credits. Payment amounts and agreement references never enter the public projection. Site changes invalidate existing public credits.

No payment provider or sponsor identity integration is implied, and sponsor acceptance here is a workflow state, not a binding electronic contract. Store private deal terms only at authorized endpoints through an authenticated E2EE channel. Production workflows require payment verification, signed agreements, refunds and tax treatment, transparency and actual UI.
